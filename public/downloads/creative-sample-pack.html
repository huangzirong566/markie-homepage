01 · Hydration UGC · 20 seconds

Objective: make a routine product useful without unsupported health promises.

TimeSpoken copyVisual / on-screen direction
0–3s“My afternoon crash was starting before lunch.”Creator drops a sachet next to a half-finished bottle. Text: “The 11:47 slump.”
3–8s“So I made one tiny rule: water first, then another coffee.”Tear, pour, shake. Three clean macro cuts.
8–15s“This lives in my bag because it takes ten seconds and actually tastes light.”Bag POV, commuting cut, one honest sip reaction. Avoid medical claims.
15–20s“If plain water keeps losing, make it easier to choose.”Product and bottle on desk. CTA: “Build the easier habit.”

04 · Mandarin adaptation sample

Literal English intent

“You don’t need more motivation. You need a lower-friction start.”

A direct translation sounds abstract in casual Chinese creator content.

Natural spoken Mandarin

“别再等状态了，先把第一步变简单。”

Back-translation: “Stop waiting to feel ready—make the first step easier.”
