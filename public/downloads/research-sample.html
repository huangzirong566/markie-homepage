QA checklist

  • Every row has an accessible source and check date.
  • Unknown values remain unknown—never silently converted to zero.
  • Factory, trading company and brand owner are not interchangeable.
  • Published capability does not become a quality or compliance claim.
  • The handoff states what still needs commercial due diligence.
